NYT Archive Crawler

Clues Puzzles Recs Quiz

What This Does

This tool crawls your NYT crossword archive to find puzzles you haven't solved yet, and imports them into the training system so you can practice with their clues.

It runs entirely in your browser using your NYT subscription - no credentials are stored on our servers.

Step 1: Add the Bookmarklet

Drag this button to your bookmarks bar:

Import NYT Puzzles

If you can't see your bookmarks bar, press Cmd+Shift+B (Mac) or Ctrl+Shift+B (Windows).

Step 2: Run the Crawler

  1. Go to the NYT Crossword Archive
    Open nytimes.com/crosswords/archive/daily and make sure you're logged in.
  2. Click the bookmarklet
    Click "Import NYT Puzzles" in your bookmarks bar. A panel will appear in the corner of the page.
  3. Wait for import to complete
    The crawler will scan your archive, find unsolved puzzles, and import them. This takes about 7-8 minutes for ~300 puzzles.
  4. Check your puzzles
    Once done, go to Puzzles to see all imported puzzles.
Important Notes
  • You must be logged into your NYT account with an active crossword subscription
  • Keep the browser tab open while the crawler runs
  • If interrupted, you can run it again - it will resume where it left off
  • The crawler only imports puzzles you haven't solved on NYT
Privacy

The crawler runs entirely in your browser. Your NYT credentials are never sent to our servers - we only receive the puzzle data (clues and answers).
